Platinum Hunter community guidelines

Tips, reviews and ratings are read by trophy hunters all over the world. To post, you have to accept these guidelines.

  1. Respect. No insults, harassment, threats, discrimination or hate speech, and that includes against studios or players.
  2. Nothing inappropriate. No sexual or violent content, nothing that promotes illegal activities and nothing that could harm anyone.
  3. No spam or personal data. No advertising, no links to other sites and no personal data, yours or anyone else's (real name, phone number, address, accounts).
  4. Fair play. No faking trophies, cheating in the rankings, or buying, selling or sharing accounts or boosting services.
  5. Flag your spoilers. If a tip gives away the story, say so at the start.
  6. Write about the game. Tips are for earning trophies, and reviews are for talking about the game and its trophies.

What happens if they're broken

Anyone can report a tip, a review or a player. After 3 reports, the content is hidden automatically until we review it. If the guidelines are broken, we may delete what was posted and remove access to the community features, temporarily or permanently. We'll always tell you why.

If you disagree

Write to us at duk3x.dev@gmail.com and we'll look into it.

What you post

What you post is yours, but by posting it you give us permission to show it in the app. You can delete it whenever you like.
