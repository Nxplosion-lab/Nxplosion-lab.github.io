Moodcell privacy policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

This policy was written in Spanish. If the translations differ, the Spanish version prevails.

1. Controller

NXplosion Labs (Duk3X), independent developer based in Spain.
Contact: duk3x.dev@gmail.com

2. What the app does

Moodcell: Emoji Battery puts a character or a design where the battery goes and lets you customise your phone's status bar (clock, centre, battery, icons and background), along with live wallpapers and a widget. It has no accounts and no server of its own: everything you set up stays on your device.

3. Data that stays on your device

  • Battery: the app reads from Android the level, whether it is charging, voltage, current, power, temperature, charge, cycles, health, chemistry and charger type, to draw the bar, the panel and the charging animation. The graph of the last 5 minutes exists only in the phone's memory.
  • Your settings: the design, the parts, the theme, the colours, your favourites and the apps where you hide the bar are stored on the device.
  • Your background photo: if you choose a photo from your gallery as the background, it is copied inside the app so it can be drawn. It never leaves the device and is not included in backups.
  • Android backup: if you have it turned on, Android may save your Moodcell settings to your Google account so you can restore them when you change phones. This is managed by Google, and you control it in Android settings.

4. Accessibility service

Moodcell uses the Android accessibility service only so that it can show its bar on top of the status bar and hide it when needed. With it, the app reads which app is in front and the type and size of its window, to hide the bar in full screen and in the apps you choose, and detects when you tap the bar so it can open the part you tapped. It does not read what you type or the content of your screen, does not store that information and does not send it anywhere. You can turn it off whenever you like in Android's accessibility settings.

5. Location (RF mode only)

The optional RF mode draws signal coverage like a spectrum analyser. To read mobile network information (signal and cells), Android requires the location permission. Moodcell uses it only at that moment, to draw the bar while that mode is active: it does not store your location or send it. If you don't turn on RF mode, the app doesn't ask for this permission.

6. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version may show ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect the device's advertising ID, IP address (approximate location), device information and ad interaction data to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before showing personalised ads, and you can change it at any time from the app's settings. More information: How Google uses data. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.

7. Purchases

The Premium purchase is handled by Google Play. We don't receive your payment details.

8. Legal basis

  • Performance of the service you request: customising your bar, your wallpapers and your widget.
  • Your consent for the accessibility service, location in RF mode and personalised advertising (which you can withdraw at any time).

9. Retention and deletion

As there is no server and there are no accounts, we don't keep any of your data. To delete everything related to Moodcell, just clear the app's data in Android settings or uninstall it. If you have any questions, write to us at the address in section 1.

10. Processors and transfers

We use Google (AdMob and Google Play) as a data processor for advertising and purchases. Google may process some data outside the European Union under the safeguards of the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

11. Minors

Moodcell is suitable for everyone: it has no accounts, chats or content from other users, and we don't collect personal data from anyone.

If you are under the age of digital consent in your country (14 in Spain), ask a parent or guardian to decide about personalized ads. You can decline them in the consent notice or in the app's settings; the ads you see will then not be personalized.

12. Your rights

You can exercise your rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction of processing and data portability by writing to duk3x.dev@gmail.com, and lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency, Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (aepd.es).

13. Changes

If this policy changes, we will publish the new version on this page with its date.
