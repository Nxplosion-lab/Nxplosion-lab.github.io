Platinum Hunter privacy policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

This policy was written in Spanish. If the translations differ, the Spanish version prevails.

1. Controller

NXplosion Labs (Duk3X), independent developer based in Spain.
Contact: duk3x.dev@gmail.com

2. What the app does

Platinum Hunter shows the trophies of your PlayStation Network account. Most of it runs only on your device, which communicates with Sony's servers. The community features (Hunter Level and rankings, friends, chat, groups, tips, ratings and cloud backup) use our server, hosted on Google Firebase (Google Cloud, European Union region, Belgium). They are only turned on if you verify your account in the app.

3. Data that stays on your device

  • PSN credentials: you enter your username and password only on Sony's official page. The app never sees or stores them.
  • PSN session: session tokens are stored encrypted on your device, with a key protected by the Android Keystore, to keep you signed in.
  • Trophy and game cache, settings and exports: stored on your device. Files you export go wherever you choose.
  • PlayStation messages: the app reads and sends your PSN messages directly with Sony from your device; they don't go through our server. Which chats you've read is stored only on your device.
  • Background checks: if you turn on alerts, your device checks your trophies (every few hours) and your PSN message inbox (every 15 minutes) with Sony, and lets you know with local notifications. You can turn this off in Profile › Notifications.
  • Critic scores: once a week the app downloads from our website a public list of OpenCritic scores taken from Wikidata. It doesn't send any of your data.
  • Widgets and shortcuts: they show data that's already on your device (your level, platinums, latest trophy and the game you're playing).

4. Data processed by our server

Only if you verify your account (Home › Hunter Level):

  • Verification and Hunter Level: the app sends your PSN session token to the server over HTTPS. It is used at that moment to confirm that the account is yours and to read your trophies from Sony, and it is not stored. Along with those trophies, we store your online ID, your avatar, your PSN account's country, your points, level and trophy counts, your progress in each game, the hours the app estimates you took for each platinum (used only for the “Platinum in” average, without showing who) and signals to detect impossible trophies (for example, hundreds in the same second).
  • Public catalogue: names, icons and rarity of each game's trophies. It doesn't identify anyone.
  • What you post: tips, votes, game ratings (difficulty, hours and score), reviews, “Can it no longer be earned?” votes, chat and group messages, the groups you create or join, and invitations.
  • Community safety: the reports you send or receive and the people you block.
  • Alerts: a Firebase Cloud Messaging notification identifier to send you chat and group alerts, if you turn them on.
  • Cloud backup: your hunt list, your wishlist, the trophies you mark as unobtainable and your appearance settings, so you can restore them when you change phones.

Your online ID, avatar, level, points and country are visible to other users in the rankings and in the community. Your chat messages are only seen by the people in the chat or group.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect the device's advertising ID, IP address (approximate location), device information and ad interaction data to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before showing personalised ads, and you can change it at any time from the Privacy and ads menu. More information: How Google uses data. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.

6. Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

If the app closes because of an error, it sends a report to Firebase Crashlytics so we can fix it: the phone model, the Android and app version, the time of the crash, the part of the code where it happened and a random installation identifier. It doesn't include your PSN account, your trophies or your messages. It is kept for up to 90 days.

7. Purchases

Premium purchases and subscriptions are handled by Google Play. We don't receive your payment details.

8. Legal basis

  • Performance of the service you request: showing your trophies and, if you verify your account, the community features.
  • Legitimate interest in keeping the community safe and the rankings fair (cheat detection, reports and blocks).
  • Your consent for personalised advertising and alerts (which you can withdraw at any time).

9. Retention and deletion

Server data is kept for as long as you use the community features. You can delete it whenever you like in Profile › Delete my server data: your ranking, your progress, your cloud backup, your blocks and your server account are deleted, and what you wrote in groups, chats, tips and reviews becomes anonymous and hidden. You can also request this by email. Signing out deletes the tokens and the cache from your device, and uninstalling the app removes all its local data.

10. Processors and transfers

We use Google (Firebase, Cloud Functions, Cloud Messaging, Crashlytics, AdMob and Google Play) as a data processor. Server data is stored in the European Union. Google may process some data outside the EU under the safeguards of the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

11. Minors

The app is not intended for children under 16.

12. Your rights

You can exercise your rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction of processing and data portability by writing to duk3x.dev@gmail.com, and lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency, Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (aepd.es).
